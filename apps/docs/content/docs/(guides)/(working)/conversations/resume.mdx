---
title: Resume a conversation
description: Pick up an earlier conversation where it left off, give it a title, or start a new one.
---

Every conversation is saved after each turn, per project. Nothing is lost when you quit.

## Pick one up

`/resume` lists the project's other conversations by title, newest first. Type to filter by title, then press `enter`.
It opens in a [tab](/conversations/tabs) of its own, and the conversation you were in stays open beside it. The agent
picks up where it left off and remembers what was said.

```terminal
{accent|+- Resume} {muted|4 conversations} {accent|-----------------------------------------------------------------+}
{accent||} {muted|search} {accent|T}{muted|ype to filter}                                                                    {accent||}
{accent|+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+}
{accent||} {code|>} {code,bold|Flaky checkout e2e test}                                                         {muted|2h ago} {accent||}
{accent||}     {muted|2 messages · "the checkout e2e test fails every few runs, can you find out why?"}     {accent||}
{accent||}   Migrate auth to OIDC                                                         {muted|yesterday} {accent||}
{accent||}     {muted|4 messages · "plan the migration from our session cookies to OIDC"}                   {accent||}
{accent||}   Billing reconciliation job                                                      {muted|3d ago} {accent||}
{accent||}     {muted|2 messages · "how does the nightly billing reconciliation work?"}                     {accent||}
{accent||}   Speed up the CI cache                                                           {muted|1w ago} {accent||}
{accent||}     {muted|2 messages · "CI takes 14 minutes, most of it installing dependencies"}               {accent||}
{accent||                                                                                          |}
{accent|+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+}
{accent||} Enter {muted|resume ·} Up/Down {muted|move ·} PgUp/PgDn {muted|page ·} Esc {muted|close}                                 {accent||}
{accent|+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+}
```

Under each title are the number of messages and your first message. `/resume oidc` opens the list already filtered.

A conversation that worked in a [worktree](/worktrees) shows `worktree swift-running-fox` and goes back into it.

A conversation open in another Jinion, such as in a second terminal, shows `open in another jinion`. It opens in one
Jinion at a time, so that neither undoes the other's turns: picking it says where it is open. Close it there, or quit
that Jinion, and it opens here. One open in `jinion serve` can be followed with `jinion --attach` instead.

To open the last conversation straight away, start Jinion with `-c` or `--continue`:

```sh
jinion -c
```

If another Jinion has that conversation open, a new one starts and says so.

## Titles

Jinion gives each conversation a title of a few words, such as `Flaky checkout e2e test`, once the first turn ends.
Until then, the title is the first line of your first message.

As the conversation moves on, Jinion names it again, so the title keeps up with the work: when the messages you have
sent double, after 20 minutes with new ones, and when you accept a [plan](/permission-modes#plan-first).

The title shows in `/resume` and on the right of the status line.

## Rename a conversation

To give the conversation a title of your own, type `/rename` and the title:

```text
/rename checkout flake
```

Jinion says `Renamed the conversation to “checkout flake”.` A title you give stays: Jinion doesn't rename the
conversation again.

`/rename` alone has Jinion name the conversation now from what it is about, and it goes on naming it as the
conversation moves on.

## Start a new one

`/clear`, or `/new`, saves the current conversation and starts a new one. What runs in the
[background](/background-tasks) stops.
