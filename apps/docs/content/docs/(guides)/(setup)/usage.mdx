## See your limits

`/usage` opens full screen with three parts:

- **Plan limits**: the 5-hour window and the week, and the week per model where your plan has one, with when each
  resets.
- **This session**: its cost at API prices, time spent, lines changed, and tokens by model.
- **What adds to your limits**: over the last day or week (`d` and `w`), the kinds of sessions and the skills,
  subagents, plugins and MCP servers that used the most.

If your plan has extra usage, the plan limits show how much of it you used this month.

<Callout>
  The cost is what the session would cost at API prices. A subscription doesn't bill it; it shows how much you used.
</Callout>

## See every day of use

Press `tab` in `/usage`, or type `/stats`, for a calendar of every day you used it, a square per day, shaded by how
much you did, like GitHub's contribution graph.

| Key            | Does                                                          |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Arrow keys     | Pick a day to see its messages, sessions and tokens           |
| `r`            | Switch between All time, Last 30 days and Last 7 days         |

Below the calendar: your favorite model, tokens, sessions, longest session, active days, longest and current streak,
most active day, and each model's share of the tokens.

The stats cover every Claude conversation on this machine, under all your [accounts](/models-and-accounts), including
ones you had outside Jinion.

`/usage` is about the agent the conversation is on. On Codex it shows your ChatGPT plan's 5-hour and weekly limits;
Codex has no Stats tab yet.

## Keep an eye on it while you work

The [status line](/status-line) can show what is left of the plan limits (`5h 92% left · 7d 80% left`), the context (`ctx [====------] 41%`)
and the session's cost all the time.
