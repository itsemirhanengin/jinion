A new layout in two modes, Agent and Code, switched from the title bar (⌘E). Agent has the threads on the left, compact
and grouped by month with a search, their tabs in the middle and cards on the right for the threads at work, the
changes, the preview and the terminals. Code has the files, search and Git, and asks about the lines you select from a
bar under them. Projects move into a menu in the title bar, with a search in its middle (⌘K). A new thread opens on where
the project stands: what waits on you, what runs, what changed since you were last here and your activity. The composer
is a capsule that grows into a box as you write, and Profile picks the accent color: Navy, Ocean, Plum, Clay or
Graphite.
