Plans of their own. A plan opens as a tab from the conversation, the panel under it or the right panel: a bar over it
says where it stands and answers it, Keep planning or Build in a mode, and the plan below is a document you can change
before you answer, with headings, marks over a selection, lists and task lists, code, tables, and Mermaid diagrams
drawn, their text edited on a click. A plan you changed goes to the agent with a line saying you changed it, so it
builds your version. Diagrams are drawn in the conversation too, and the agent is asked to write plans as a short
document with a diagram, steps, the files and the risks. The demo agent plans when asked to.
